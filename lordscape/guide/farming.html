Guide

Farming guide

How growing works

Crops grow in real time, even while you're logged out. Each kind of crop grows a stage every cycle:

Cycle Crops
5 minutes flowers
10 minutes allotments, hops and fibre, kelp
20 minutes herbs, bushes
40 minutes trees, glowcaps
80 minutes sandthorn, silverleaf
160 minutes fruit trees, staffwood
320 minutes the waytree
640 minutes hardwoods, the elderwood, the blightseed, the Word seeds

So potatoes take 40 minutes, herbs 80, an oak about three hours, a fruit tree 16 hours and a linden about three days.

Stages end on growth ticks of a shared clock, shifted by up to half an hour for each player. Crops of a kind planted on the same run ripen together, so plant a run's herbs one after another and come back for them all at once.

The chores

  • Rake the weeds out of a patch (a Rake). An untouched patch is overgrown.
  • Plant seeds with a Seed dibber: three in an allotment, four hops, one herb or flower. Trees go in as saplings, planted with a Spade.
  • Compost the patch, before or while it grows: Compost, Rich compost or Ember compost. Each tier adds a harvest and cuts disease.
  • Water allotments, hops and flowers with a Watering can. It holds eight pours: fill it at a well, trough or pump. Watering halves that stage's chance of disease.
  • Harvest when it's grown: allotments need a spade. A patch gives at least three harvests (more with compost), and each harvest may not use one up. Your chance of that rises with your Farming level.
  • Trees, bushes and fruit trees: Check-health first (most of their xp), then chop, pick or clear.

Disease

Every stage after the first may sicken: herbs 27 times in 128, fruit trees 18, the better trees less. Compost halves it, rich compost cuts it to a fifth, ember compost to a tenth.

A diseased crop stops growing and dies at the next stage unless cured: - a Plant cure for allotments, herbs, flowers, hops and hardwoods; - Secateurs for trees, fruit trees and bushes (three tries in four).

To keep a crop safe: - Pay a gardener (Watch-crops). Each crop has its price, in produce, and notes count. Gardeners won't watch herbs or flowers. - Grow a flower beside it. A grown flower guards the allotments next to it: - Cornflower guards potatoes, onions and carrots; - Tansy guards cabbages; - Moonpetal guards beetroot and leeks; - Bellflower guards pumpkins; - the Vigil lily (58) guards every allotment; - a Scarecrow (23) stood in a flower patch guards beetroot. - Disease-free patches: - the Herbalists' Guild's herb beds, for members; - Tideholm's herb bed, after The Tide's Answer, which also gives longer harvests.

Compost bins

Fill a bin with 15 weeds, scraps or produce, close it, and come back 35 to 70 minutes later. Open it and take the compost out a sack at a time: - weeds and produce under 47 make compost; - a bin of nothing but high-level produce (pumpkins, fruit from plums up, herbs from tarnroot up) makes rich compost; - 25 Volcanic ash on a shut bin of rich compost makes ember compost (Farming 50). The ash is mined from ash heaps at the Firekeepers' Hold and on Emberpeak.

A bin of nothing but carrots rots into spoilt carrots.

Potting sheds

Every set of patches has a potting shed: - Store your tools and compost in it. What one shed holds, every shed holds. - While you work the patches beside any shed, its tools count as carried. - Note turns your produce into bank notes.

Runs

Patches stand in sets across every land: Hollow Farm, Stillwater, Applecombe, Athelbury's green, the woodcutters' camp, the Herbalists' Guild, Tideholm, Saltreach, the stilt village, Cragmoot, the riders' camp, the canyon city, Brinehook, Greenshroud (hardwoods), the Firekeepers' Hold and the Growers' Guild. A herb run or a tree run visits each in turn.

The Glebe

The Church's fields below the cathedral, from Farming 34. 1. Take seeds at the table (the best you can grow). 2. Plant them in the beds and water each one before each of its first three minutes is up, or it wilts. 3. Harvest the gourds and load them into the almonry cart for xp. The 75th gourd in the cart pays a lump on top.

You earn a glebe mark for every three gourds. Reeve Oda's rewards: - the grower's garb (2.5% more Farming xp for all four pieces); - the everfull can (1,000 pours); - the seed and herb satchels; - silvered secateurs (more from every harvest); - the weed-ward blessing.

The Growers' Guild

South of Applecombe, in three courts for Farming 45, 65 and 85. Guildmistress Rowan Tilly gives contracts: grow a crop in the guild's own beds and she pays a seed pack. - The middle court has the warded bed for a Blightseed. Grown, it wakes the Blightbloom: burst its buds, then beat it for 12,600 Farming xp and its seeds. - Among those seeds are the Word seeds, grown one at a time in the Word bed: - the Stone seed keeps disease off; - the Tide seed lengthens harvests; - the Gale seed hurries crops on.

The pet

Burr turns up now and then when you check a tree's health or finish a patch, more often the higher your level, and from the Blightbloom.
