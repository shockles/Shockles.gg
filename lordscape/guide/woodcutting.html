Woodcutting guide

How chopping works

You swing every 4 ticks (2.4 seconds), whatever hatchet you hold. A better hatchet doesn't swing faster. It makes each swing likelier to bring a log away, and the higher your level, the likelier still. Each tree has a timer:

  • It only runs down while someone is chopping it, and only once a tick, however many are chopping.
  • Left alone, it grows back.
  • When it runs out, the tree falls on the next log.

Firs and pines give one log and fall. You use the best hatchet you have the level for, from your pack or your hand.

What turns up

  • Birds' nests. One log in 256 knocks a bird's nest out of the tree, at your feet. Search it for seeds, a ring or an egg. Lay an egg on any altar for 100 Prayer xp and a nest of seeds.
  • Twig. A sprig of the Heartwood that walks, now and then, the rarer the tree the likelier.

Gear

All from Bryn at The Woodcutters' Camp, for heartbark (see below):

  • Woodsman's cap, jerkin, breeches and boots: 2.5% more xp for all four (Woodcutting 44).
  • Log basket: open it and 28 logs go in it instead of your pack.
  • Felling haft: smithed onto any hatchet. With Woodsman's rations in your pack you get 10% more xp, but one log in five splits to nothing.
  • Woodward's offering: with Woodcutting 68 and Farming 50, about half the time the tree gives a second log, and four times in five with ten of you on it.

The Ember hatchet burns a third of its logs as you cut them, for half their Firemaking xp. It needs Firemaking 85. It's reforged at a lava forge from an umbrite hatchet and a Cinder of Ashmaw.

The woods stir

Chop any tree past the firs and, every minute or two, the woods stir round it. Everyone who was chopping nearby takes part; latecomers get a token share. Each stirring pays Woodcutting xp and heartbark.

Chopping the same tree with others gives each of you a hidden +1 Woodcutting per chopper, up to +10.

The Woodcutters' Guild

North-west of The Heartwood. You need Woodcutting 60 to get in. Inside, every chop is as if you had 7 more levels. It has:

  • stands of every tree;
  • two elderwoods, chopped from the platforms of their scaffolds (climb the ladders);
  • Tobin the sawyer's sawmill (logs to planks, for coins);
  • Quartermaster Ayla's hatchets.

The root hollow goes down to The Rootdeep, where Oakenwards walk. A felled oakenward leaves its trunk, and chopping it gives noted logs of the best tree you can cut.

Canoes

Canoe stations stand on the Ash at Stillwater, Ashwick, Millford and the Stilt Village:

  1. Chop down the alder (Woodcutting 12).
  2. Shape the best canoe your level allows. That gives the xp, and decides how far it goes.
  3. Paddle there.
