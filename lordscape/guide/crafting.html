Guide

Crafting guide

Crafting is everything made by hand that isn't forged: gems cut, jewellery cast, hides tanned and sewn, wool and flax spun and woven, clay thrown and fired, glass blown, staves fixed.

How making works

Most things are made in hand, a go every 3 ticks; gems cut faster (2 ticks) and staves slower (4). Things made at a station (a furnace, a loom, a potter's wheel, a kiln, a spinning wheel, a tanning rack) open its window; pick what to make and how many.

Thread. Sewing leather and hide takes a length of thread a piece, not a whole yarn: a Yarn is five lengths, and what's left over is kept for the next piece. The Tailor's needle wants no thread at all.

Gems

Rough gems come from mining (now and then, more with a lodestone amulet), from gem rocks, from creatures and from the jewellers' shops. Cut them with a Chisel.

A split stone leaves a Crushed gem (a little xp; grind five into sand). The Jeweller's chisel makes agate, jasper and carnelian sure from 62, 71 and 76, and one cut in ten gives two stones.

Jewellery

Cast at a furnace with a mould: a ring, necklace, bracelet (worn on the hands) or amulet. Agate, jasper and carnelian are set in silver, the rest in gold. Amulets come out of the mould unstrung: Use a yarn on one to string it (4 xp).

Leather and hides

Hides are tanned before they're sewn: free at a Tanning rack, one at a time, or the whole pack at once at a tanner (1 coin a cowhide, 2 a wolf pelt, 5 troll hide or sealskin, 15 lurker hide, 20 a drake's). Tanning gives no xp.

Each drake tier is vambraces (one leather), chaps (two), a shield (two and an oak plank) and a body (three). A body is the fastest Crafting xp there is.

Pottery

Use clay on water (a well, a trough) to work it soft, shape it on a potter's wheel, fire it in a kiln: pot 1, cup 3, pie dish 7, bowl 8, jug 10, plant pot 19, pot lid 25. Fill a plant pot with earth (a trowel) to plant a tree seed in it.

Glass

Burn kelp on any fire for kelp ash; melt sand and kelp ash at a furnace into molten glass (20 xp). Blow it with a pipe: drinking glass 1, lantern 4, oil lamp 12, lantern glass 26, vial 33, glass float 42, glass orb 46, lantern lens 49, light orb 87.

Staves

A glass orb charged at an altar (Magic) and fixed to an oak staff: tide 54, stone 58, ember 62, gale 66; the bound staves 70-76.

The Crafthall

At Millford on the Ash: Crafting 40 and a Crafter's apron on to get in (Hedda Loom sells them at the gate). Inside: potter's wheels, a kiln, a furnace, spinning wheels, looms, a tanner, clay in the yard and the guild's tools to take. Mistress Orla Penrose's orders pay coins and xp; every 25th pays a reward: the jeweller's chisel, then the tailor's needle, then the crafter's garb (2.5% more Crafting xp for the four pieces).

The Delvings' lamps (52)

Lampwright Tuva of the Delvers' Hall pays crafters to mend the Delvings' broken lamps with light orbs: 500 Crafting and 500 Firemaking a lamp (both 52 to do it), and a bonus every fifty.

Clay wardens (60)

The stilt village in the Soddens raises clay wardens: shape one from ten soft clay on a wheel, fire it, cut a Word of Will into its brow, and stand it cloaked on a plinth. Each one earns a fen token from Elder Fenna's stores.
