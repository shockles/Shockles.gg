Guide

Herblore guide

Herblore is brewing: herbs cleaned, woken in water and given their secondary, three doses to a vial. It opens with A Bitter Remedy (Wenna in Ashwick), which leaves you at level 3.

How brewing works

Cleaning a grimy herb takes a tick, mixing it into a vial of water a tick (no xp), and adding the secondary two ticks. Making goes on until you run out, your pack fills, or something hits you. Many secondaries are crushed first with a Pestle and mortar: horn or tusk to Horn dust, a drake scale to Scale dust, an empty bird's nest to a Crushed nest, drakestone to Drakestone dust, wyrm-king bones, and echostone to Echo dust.

Doses. A brew makes three doses (some make four). Use one potion on another of its kind to pour them together, up to four. Ottilie (the Herbalists' Guild) and Wenna decant a whole pack, noted potions too, into vials of whatever size you like, free. A Stillroom amulet (Enchant Agate, Magic 27) now and then brews four doses instead of three; it has five charges.

Ottilie cleans grimy herbs for 200 coins each (noted ones from Herblore 70). Wordbloom also grows wild round the highest Wordfalls' craters.

Training

  • 3-30: keen draughts, antidotes, might draughts; Brannagh's tea at 18 (a cup of hot water, embersage, two hearthwort and a fenmint) is good money.
  • 30-45: warding draughts, battle draughts (36), prayer draughts (38).
  • 45-63: greater keen (45), greater antidotes (48), greater might (55), vigil draughts (58).
  • 63-90: hallowed restores (63), greater warding (66), hawkeye (72), seer's (76), the brews (78-85).
  • 90+: the Titan's brew: a greater keen, might and warding draught (four doses each) and a wordbloom.

About 2,500 potions an hour, banking.

Steadfast potions (70-97): a greater draught, hawkeye, seer's or titan's brew with echo dust, a pinch a dose. They cost 10 health to drink and hold their boost for five minutes without easing back.

Weapon venom (60, 73, 82) coats a weapon: 4, 5 or 6 poison on blows, 2, 3 or 4 on shots (a dose coats five shots).

The riverfolk's mixes

From Herblore 4, Old Wade at Millford's weir teaches you to tip a roe (caviar past level 24) into a two-dose potion: a mix, drunk like food, that does what the potion does and heals 3 (roe) or 6 (caviar) a dose.

The Concordance

From Herblore 60, in the Herbalists' Guild's garden. Press clean herbs at the press into three pastes by humour (hearth: the low herbs; still: tarnroot, duskfennel, brightwort; bright: stormthistle and up). Take three orders at the vats, mix each, and work it at the station its order names:

  • the still: Work it every tick and it goes twice as fast;
  • the crucible: Work it as the bellows open the fourth time;
  • the shaker: Work it as it rattles (twice).

Hand in two at once for a fifth more xp and resin, three for two fifths. A rootling pulled from the beds doubles one. Ottilie's rewards: the Stillroom lenses (a secondary saved one time in ten), the Simples pouch (26 of each secondary), the Distiller's amulet (four doses 15% of the time) and the stillroom garb (2.5% more xp, all four).
