Firemaking guide

How lighting works

Use Light on logs in your pack, with Flint and steel in it. The logs go down at your feet and you strike every 2 ticks until they catch. Every log is lit on the same odds, which depend only on your Firemaking level:

  • At level 1 about one strike in four catches.
  • From level 43 every strike catches.

A log only sets the level it opens at and the xp it gives. When the fire catches you step west (east, south or north if that's blocked), ready to light the next. Once you never miss, a line of fires burns about 1,485 logs an hour.

Every fire burns for 100 to 198 ticks (a minute to two), whatever the logs, then leaves Ashes. Logs you walked away from can be lit where they lie: right-click them, Light.

You can't light a fire: - indoors, in a doorway, or in front of a door; - on a bridge or causeway; - in a bank, a busy town square or a guild's yard; - in Emberwatch, where only the watchfires burn.

Bonfires

Use logs on any fire, or Tend it, and it becomes a Bonfire. You put a log on every 9 ticks for its full xp, lowest-level logs first, and keep going until you run out. That's slower than a line of fires (about 975 logs an hour against 1,485), but you never move.

  • Each log adds a little to the bonfire's burn, up to 3 minutes ahead.
  • Check says how it's burning.
  • Anyone can feed anyone's bonfire, and each feeder gets their own xp.
  • You can't start a new bonfire within 5 tiles of another.

The Camp hearths at The Riders' Camp, The Hunters' Camp, The Woodcutters' Camp and on Saltreach's quay take logs the same way, and never go out.

Emberwatch

From Firemaking 50. On the Frostmark plateau, north of the Rimevault's road, the Firekeepers hold back the Stillfrost, a cold that takes the voice first, with four watchfires round its rift. Anyone in the court is in the round. A minute after the last one ends, it wakes:

  • Light a watchfire with flint and steel: 25 points and 6 × your Firemaking level in xp.
  • Chop frostroots at the stumps by each watchfire (any hatchet: Woodcutting xp), and Fletch them into frost kindling with a knife (Fletching xp).
  • Feed the watchfire: a root is 10 points and 3 × your level in xp; kindling is 25 points and 3.8 × your level. Every root and bundle wears the Stillfrost down, and so does every lit watchfire.
  • The Stillfrost snuffs watchfires out, or shatters them. Stand clear of the ground it marks. Mend a shattered one with a hammer (25 points, Construction xp).
  • Snow falls on marked ground round you. Step off it.
  • Each watchfire's kindler freezes as the round goes on. Help him with a dose of Warming draught (Pick frostbloom by the walls, mix it into a vial of meltwater from the supply crate) before he falls. If he falls, his watchfire goes out and won't light until you help him. Helping one is 75 points.
  • The cold. It takes your warmth: harder the higher your Firemaking, less for each warm thing you wear (up to four), less beside a lit watchfire. Warm things are fur, the Kindler's hood and the rest of the garb, Lined mittens, and a lit torch or lantern. A meal that heals 4 or more gives warmth back, and so does a warming draught. At no warmth, the cold kills you.

When the Stillfrost is beaten, everyone with 500 points or more gets 100 × their Firemaking level in xp and rolls on the Firekeepers' coffer, straight to the bank: - one roll for every 500 points; - a chance at one more for what's over (1,200 points: two rolls and a 40% chance of a third).

The coffer gives supplies scaled to your own levels (logs, ores, gems, herbs, seeds, fish, coins), burnt leaves and fire salts. Its rarer things:

The Keepers' pyre

By The Drowned Chapel the Keepers burn the drowned dead: 1. Anoint logs with Chrism (holy water and ember moss, Herblore 20) to make pyre logs. 2. Lay Drowned remains (from the crypt's drowned dead) on the Keepers' pyre and Cremate them.

Each cremation gives the pyre logs' Firemaking xp and the remains' Prayer xp. About one in four leaves a Reliquary key in the ashes, for the crypt's sealed niches.

Lights

Lights are made, then lit with flint and steel at a Firemaking level. A lit light keeps the dark off you underground and counts as warm.

Fire salts

A pinch of Verdigris salt, Cobalt salt, Violet salt, White salt or Crimson salt on fir logs makes logs that always catch and burn in that colour. 250 of a salt fed to Sparkwing turns its feathers that colour.
