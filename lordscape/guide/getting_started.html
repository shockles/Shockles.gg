Getting started

You wake up in Ashwick with nothing to your name.

  • Tools first. Maren's Market Stall on Ashwick's square sells a Bronze hatchet, a Bronze pickaxe, a Small net and a Fishing rod, so you can start gathering straight away.
  • A first quest. Old Hale in Ashwick has lost something precious: The Stolen Brooch.
  • First fights. Grey wolves prowl where the west road meets the forest. Look up any creature's drops and stats by right-clicking it in game, or on its page here.
  • Banking. Anything with a Bank option opens your bank; notes, tabs and placeholders work as you'd expect.
