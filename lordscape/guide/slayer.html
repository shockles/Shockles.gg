Guide

Slayer guide

Slayer is hunting to order. A Slayer master sets you a task, to kill a number of one kind of creature. Every kill on task gives Slayer xp equal to the creature's hitpoints, and finishing the task pays points to spend on rewards. Some creatures can only be fought at a Slayer level, and many need a trick: something to wear, an item to finish them with, or the right kind of weapon.

The rules for points: - No points at first. Points start from your fifth finished task. - Milestones. Every 10th task in a row pays ×5, every 50th ×15, every 100th ×25, every 250th ×35 and every 1,000th ×50. - Tamsin never pays. Her tasks still count for your streak. She'll also swap another master's task for one of hers, but your streak goes back to nothing. - The Wayfinder sets every task at a place, and only kills there count. Kills on his tasks sometimes drop a Sandseal key for his coffer. - Captain Thorne's tasks count only on the Drift's and the Drownings' waters and isles. She keeps a streak of her own.

Rewards

Ask any master for Rewards. The window has four tabs: - Unlock: - the Hunter's helm (sewn at Crafting 55); - the Hunter's ring (cast at Crafting 75); - broad arrows and bolts; - superiors and boss tasks; - the finishers (salt, water and plant cure used for you); - task storage, an extra block, and the jade sentinels and drowned court as tasks; - the helm's colours, and hallowing a helm. - Extend: longer tasks of a kind you like. - Buy: the tracker's mask, the hunter's ring, the Sigil pouch and more. - Tasks: - skip your task for 30 points (your streak is kept); - block a kind for good, at that master's price; - store a task.

You get one block slot at Slayer 1, and more at 40, 60, 80, 90 and 99. You can boost your Slayer level to fight a creature, but not to be given it as a task.

The tracker's mask and the hunter's helm

The Tracker's mask makes your melee a sixth more accurate and harder against your task. It does nothing for arrows or spells. The Stalker's cowl adds 15% for those too.

The Hunter's helm is sewn from the mask (or the cowl), a Hush hood, a Censer kerchief, a Sand veil, a Clinger cap and a Tally stone. It keeps off everything those did, and it defends like a brinesteel helm. Hallowed, by the masters or at the high altar, it adds 15% to arrows and spells on task.

Superiors

With Bigger and badder, one kill in 200 on task may raise a superior beside you. Only you can fight it. It gives ten times its hitpoints in Slayer xp and three rolls of its kind's loot. Now and then it drops a Kindled heart or a bound staff, and very rarely the Unfading stone, which makes a hunter's ring everlasting.

Boss tasks

With Like a boss, Vesna, the Wayfinder and Captain Thorne may set you a boss: you choose which, and how many (3 to 35). Each kill counts, and you get 5,000 Slayer xp on top when the task is done. It's the one time your helm helps against a boss.

Partners

Right-click another player and choose Hunt with. If they do the same, you share tasks: kills on a shared task count for both of you. Rub your tally stone's Partner to stop.
