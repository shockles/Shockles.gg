Guide

Fletching guide

How making works

Everything fletched is made in the hand: use a Whittling knife on logs, feathers on shafts, a string on an unstrung bow. Once you start, you keep going, set after set, until:

  • you run out of something;
  • your pack is full;
  • you do something else (click a creature, a thing, an item, a spell; walking is fine);
  • or something hits you.

Each kind of work takes its own time:

Work Ticks a go
Cutting a bow, a stock, shafts or a staff 3
Stringing a bow or crossbow 2
Hammering limbs onto a stock 2
Feathering shafts, tipping arrows (15 at a time) 2
Tipping bolts with gem tips (10 at a time) 2
Feathering darts and bolts (10 at a time) 1
Cutting bolt tips from a gem (12 a gem) 5
Wooden shields 7

The Bowyer's knife, carried or held, takes a tick off every cut after the first. The String spool holds your bowstrings (250, more when spools are wound together) and strings bows straight off it, so a pack holds 27 unstrung bows instead of 14 bows and 14 strings.

Bows

Every bow is cut from logs, then strung with a Bowstring (spun from flax at a spinning wheel). Cutting and stringing pay the same.

Shortbows shoot every 4 ticks and reach 7 tiles; longbows every 6 and reach 10. A wood's two bows are equally accurate. Bows add no strength: the arrows do.

Shafts and arrows

A knife on logs makes Arrow shafts: 15 from fir, 30 from oak, 45 from willow or linden, 60 from greywood, 75 from duskpine, 90 from firstwood, 105 from elderwood. Feathers on shafts make headless arrows (1 xp each); arrowheads on those make arrows, 15 at a time. Arrowheads are smithed, 15 to a bar.

Darts and bolts

Dart tips and unfinished bolts are smithed, 10 to a bar; a feather each finishes them, ten at a time, a set every tick. Darts pay far better than bolts. Gem bolt tips are cut with a chisel (12 a gem: garnet at 31, lapis at 51, moonstone at 64) and fixed to steel, stormsilver and umbrite bolts.

Crossbows

A crossbow takes three steps: a stock cut from the right wood, the smithed limbs hammered on, then a Crossbow string, spun at a spinning wheel from Sinew (raw beef dried on a range). A bowstring won't do.

Shields and staves

Two logs and a knife make a wooden shield: oak at 27, willow 42, linden 47, greywood 57, bog oak 62, duskpine 72, firstwood 87, elderwood 92. They turn arrows well and leave the other hand for a crossbow. The Elderwood walking staff is cut at 90.

Blowpipes

A blowpipe fires darts loaded into it (wield darts with the blowpipe in hand, or use them on it), every 3 ticks, reaching 5 tiles; its own strength adds to the darts'.

Sea serpents drop swordfin bills; the whalers' store on Ribhaven sells a few. The thornpipe is charged with Creeper sap (a third of its shots spend none), envenoms a quarter of what it hits, and its special, Thorn Siphon, heals you by half of what it hits.

The Mossback Posts

South of the Eldwood, past the hunters' lodge, eight post sites stand in a ring on the mossbacks' round. Wenlock Bramble, the post-wright, starts you off (The Carver's Mark, Fletching 20).

  1. Build a post at an empty site with a log you have the level for.
  2. Carve three beasts on it: the three whose spirits are about that site now. Each wrong one costs a quarter of the dressing's xp and much of the offerings.
  3. Decorate it with four things fletched from the same wood: bows, unstrung or strung, stocks, shields (elderwood: walking staves).

The mossbacks walk the ring, stopping at each post to admire it and leaving offerings at its foot. Collect them and Sort them a hundred at a time, for a lore point and a find each (logs, strings, feathers, seeds, and now and then a rare one). Step in both of a mossback's tracks on the trail and its next visit to one of your posts leaves twice as much.

Wenlock's exchange trades lore for the String spool (250), the Bowyer's knife (350), the Mossback mask (500) and the Mossback sprig (20).
