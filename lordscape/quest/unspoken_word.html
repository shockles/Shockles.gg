Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Senna, Ashwick's hedge-witch, knows a tale of a Word no one may speak: the one Unwyrd reached for when he fell. She keeps a doorstep by the Lamplit Sigil. (A long road: I must know the Words first, and the goblins of the Ashmarch's warcamp must be faced.)
  2. Stage 1 Senna thinks the oldest of the words is hidden at the Will altar, in the Fallen Seminary on its hill north-east of Ashwick. I should search the altar.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Worn tablet I found half of a slate tablet beneath the Will altar, cut with rows of marks. I should show it to Senna in Ashwick.
  4. Stage 2 Senna couldn't read the tablet. She says the elven Wardens have a loremaster who might. Their holdfast faces the goblins' warcamp in the Ashmarch, east of Athelbury.
  5. Stage 3 Loremaster Ithiel says the tablet teaches the old words, and the last of them, the Unspoken Word, is on the missing half. The goblin firecallers at the warcamp wear its shards as charms. I need to take one from them.
  6. Stage 3, carrying Tablet shard I have a shard of the tablet that fits the worn half. I should bring both pieces to Loremaster Ithiel at the Wardens' holdfast.
  7. Stage 4 Loremaster Ithiel taught me to pray the Unspoken Word, never to say it: Wrath, the Word Ordan kept back. The Wrath altar on the Silent Amphitheatre's floor, in the Unsaid, will answer me now, I can call on the Wrath spells, and the Wardens' mages will trade with me. QUEST COMPLETE.
