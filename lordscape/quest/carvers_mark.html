Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Wenlock Bramble, a post-wright in the south of the Eldwood, watches the mossbacks walk the wood. (Fletching 20.)
  2. Stage 1 Wenlock wants me to build an oak post at one of the empty sites, carve on it the three beasts I see about it, and dress it with four things fletched from oak.
  3. Stage 2 My first post stands carved and dressed. I should tell Wenlock.
  4. Stage 3 Wenlock showed me how to tend the Mossback Posts: build, carve and dress posts for the mossbacks, collect their offerings and sort them, a hundred at a time. QUEST COMPLETE.
