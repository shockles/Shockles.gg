Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Wenna Marsh, Ashwick's apothecary, looks worried about something. I could ask her what's wrong. (It could start me on Herblore.)
  2. Stage 1 Wenna needs three handfuls of nettles (they grow by Hollow Farm's fields; gloves save stings) and a wolf's fang (the grey wolves where the west road meets the forest) for a keen draught.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Wolf fang I have a wolf's fang for Wenna. She also wants three handfuls of nettles.
  4. Stage 2 I helped Wenna brew a keen draught for the well-keeper's boy, and she taught me the start of Herblore: clean a grimy herb, steep it in a vial of water, then add what wakes it. QUEST COMPLETE.
