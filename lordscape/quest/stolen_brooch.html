Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Old Hale of Ashwick looks troubled. I could ask him what's wrong. (There may be brigands involved: some combat experience would help.)
  2. Stage 1 Brigands robbed Old Hale on the Soddens road and took his late wife's silver brooch. Maren, the trader in Ashwick, hears about everything that's bought and sold. I should ask her.
  3. Stage 2 A brigand called Rook tried to sell Maren the brooch. He runs with the brigands at Cutpurse Dell, in the woods south-west of Ashwick, off the Soddens road past the Ash.
  4. Stage 2, carrying Silver brooch I found the silver brooch at the old seminary. I should take it back to Old Hale in Ashwick.
  5. Stage 2, carrying Rook's note I took a note off Rook. It says the brooch is hidden in a crack at the foot of a pillar in the ruined seminary on the hill north-east of Ashwick.
  6. Stage 3 I returned the silver brooch to Old Hale. QUEST COMPLETE.
