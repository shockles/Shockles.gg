Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Captain Vale of the Athelbury Watch keeps the Athel Keep, frowning down the east road. (A long road: mining, smithing and hard fighting. Iron will be needed.)
  2. Stage 1 Captain Vale won't send me against the Red Hands without iron on my back. There's an iron seam at the south end of Ashwick's quarry, and a furnace and anvil at its camp. An iron helm, chainmail or kiteshield will do.
  3. Stage 2 The captain wants proof I can fight: five red armbands from the Red Hands at Thornwall, the hill fort out in the Ashmarch.
  4. Stage 3 Now for Vorrin Redhand himself, in his war hall down the stairs under the keep at the heart of Thornwall. The captain wants his seal as proof. I should take food, and step back when he raises his axe.
  5. Stage 4 Vorrin Redhand is dead and his seal is on the captain's desk. The road east is a little safer. QUEST COMPLETE.
