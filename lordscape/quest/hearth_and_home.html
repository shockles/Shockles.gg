Hearth and Home

Hearth and Home
StartAldous Plumb, master builder
LocationAshwick
Quest points1

Rewards

  • 1 quest point
  • 500 Construction xp
  • Plank × 10
  • Nails × 20
  • Hearth tablet × 3

Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Aldous Plumb, the master builder by the Hearthgate just outside Ashwick's south gate, seems to want someone to teach. I could talk to him. (Construction starts here.)
  2. Stage 1 Aldous gave me a saw, a hammer, two fir logs, nails and a block of stone. I must saw the logs into two planks at the sawbench beside the Hearthgate and show him.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Plank I've a plank sawn. Aldous wants to see two.
  4. Stage 2 Aldous liked my planks. Now I go through the Hearthgate in Build mode (right-click it) into my own garden and build on one of its empty spaces: a planter box (a plank and a nail) or a sundial (a plank and a stone block).
  5. Stage 3 I built something in my own garden. I should go and tell Aldous, by the Hearthgate outside Ashwick's south gate.
  6. Stage 4 Aldous took me on as a builder. He told me the Hearthgate's name, too: with Magic 28 I can cast Hearthgate Call, and go home from anywhere. QUEST COMPLETE.
