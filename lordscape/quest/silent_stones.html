Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Ruan, elder of the Windmoor's horse folk, keeps looking north from the riders' camp, toward the Wending Stones. (Brigands are involved: some combat experience would help.)
  2. Stage 1 The Wending Stones used to hum on still nights, and the riders knew a humming night meant fair weather for the herds. Since brigands camped below them the stones have been silent, and storms have caught the herds. Ruan asked me to look at the ring, on the hill north of the camp.
  3. Stage 2 Someone has chiselled a mark off one of the Wending Stones, and fresh chips lead down to the brigands' camp below the hill. Ruan thinks it was Hask the Chisel, their leader, once a mason. I should take the mark back from him.
  4. Stage 2, carrying Carved mark I took the stone's carved mark from Hask the Chisel. I should set it back into the scarred stone in the ring.
  5. Stage 3 The mark is back in its stone, and the Wending Stones hum again. I should tell Ruan at the riders' camp.
  6. Stage 4 The Wending Stones hum over the Windmoor again. Ruan says a stranger in grey, who never spoke a word, paid the brigands in silver. QUEST COMPLETE.
