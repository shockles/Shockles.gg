Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Brother Oswin, a hermit of the Conclave, keeps a cell by Orison Tarn, high in the fells north-west of Athelbury. He listens for the words Ordan spoke, and something troubles him. (Goblins will have to be faced.)
  2. Stage 1 Brother Oswin says the words he listens for are going quiet across the high country. To hear where the silence comes from he needs a listening bowl, turned from the heart of a great oak. A great bough has fallen in the Heartwood, the ring of great oaks deep in the Eldwood.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Heartwood I cut a length of heartwood from the fallen bough. Bryn's woodcutters, on the Eldwood's west road, can turn it into a bowl.
  4. Stage 1, carrying Listening bowl The woodcutters turned the heartwood into a listening bowl. I should take it to Brother Oswin at Orison Tarn.
  5. Stage 2 Oswin listened through the bowl. The silence is thickest at the goblins' camp along the fells west of the tarn. One of them carries a bell that makes no sound, and it is swallowing the words. I must take it from them.
  6. Stage 2, carrying Silent bell I took the silent bell from the goblins' bell-bearer. I should bring it to Brother Oswin.
  7. Stage 3 Brother Oswin sank the silent bell in the tarn, and the high country's words came back. The bell bore the Hollow's mark: a mouth stitched shut. QUEST COMPLETE.
