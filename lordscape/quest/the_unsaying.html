Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Waykeeper Idris tends the brazier at the ruined waystation on the high pass, east of Cragmoot. (He'll only trust someone who has finished The Silent Stones and The Listening Bowl. The Hollow must be faced, and their Prior: a road for a seasoned fighter.)
  2. Stage 1 Idris's brazier is dying however well he feeds it, and the Hold's great flame burns lower each night. He thinks the Hollow are behind it. I should search their oldest shrine, the barrow field on the Windmoor, far west past the Eldwood.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Grey ledger I found a ledger hidden in a cairn at the Windmoor's barrow field, full of crossed-out marks. I should take it to Waykeeper Idris.
  4. Stage 2 The ledger lists the words the Hollow have stolen, all sunk into an Unsaying stone. The Hushed Prior who keeps it means to say it at the Hold and put out its flame. I must kill her in her crypt at the end of the Barrowdowns, down the stair in the great barrow, and take the stone. She marks the ground before she strikes: I should step off it.
  5. Stage 2, carrying Unsaying stone I took the Unsaying stone from the Hushed Prior. I should bring it to Waykeeper Idris before anyone can say it.
  6. Stage 3 Idris cast the Unsaying stone into his brazier, and every stolen word burst out of it at once. The waystation's fire roars, and the Hold's flame with it. QUEST COMPLETE.
