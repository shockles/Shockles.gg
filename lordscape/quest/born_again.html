Born Again

Born Again

LocationThe Cathedral of the First Answer

Rewards

  • 3 quest points
  • 750 Prayer xp
  • 3,000 Inscription xp
  • 3,000 Smithing xp

Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Archprelate Aldebrand, in the cathedral on its spur above Athelbury, has a lost saint's relic on his mind. (I must have ended the Hushed Prior's work in The Unsaying, and be ready to fight her again; about level 40 in Prayer, Inscription and Smithing.)
  2. Stage 1 The Hollow beat Saint Ormund's hammer-head into the crossguard of the Hushed Prior's blade. I must take it from her crypt at the end of the Barrowdowns.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Ormund's Hammer (re-forged) Ormund's hammer is re-forged. Now I must Consecrate it at the cathedral's high altar: a saint's relic, holy water, and Will and Stone sigils.
  4. Stage 1, carrying Ormund's hammer-head I have Ormund's hammer-head. It must be re-forged at an anvil (Smithing 40: two steel bars and oak logs), then consecrated at the high altar.
  5. Stage 2 Ormund's hammer is reborn. The Archprelate has given me the ledger of the saints' lost relics: I may bring holy things, lost or spoiled, to be reborn. QUEST COMPLETE.
