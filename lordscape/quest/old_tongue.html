The Old Tongue

The Old Tongue
StartHigh Magister Ysolde
LocationThe Conclave of the Word
Quest points3

Rewards

  • 3 quest points
  • 15,000 Magic xp
  • 5,000 Inscription xp
  • Old Tongue sceptre

Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 High Magister Ysolde, at the Conclave of the Word in Athelbury, studies how the old kingdoms spoke. (I'll need to have learned the Unspoken Word first, and a Magic level of 50 to use what she finds.)
  2. Stage 1 Ysolde says the old kingdoms never spoke a Word alone: they spoke them together, two at a time, as bound sigils hold them. The chorus they sang is carved on the chorus stone on the Silent Amphitheatre's floor, in the Unsaid. I should take a rubbing of it.
  3. Stage 1, carrying Chorus rubbing I took a rubbing of the chorus stone. I should bring it to Ysolde in Athelbury.
  4. Stage 2 The rubbing has four verses and the last is broken off. Ysolde thinks the Buried King, the last of the old kings, took the last verse into the Sunken Kingdom under the Scour. I'll have to take it from him.
  5. Stage 2, carrying King's verse The Buried King's last verse is mine. I should bring it to Ysolde.
  6. Stage 3 Ysolde taught me the Old Tongue: the Words spoken together. At the Will altar or the Wrath altar I can change from the Conclave's spells to the old kingdoms', and back. QUEST COMPLETE.
