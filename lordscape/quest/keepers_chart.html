Walkthrough

  1. Stage 0 Keeper Brannoc keeps the light on Lantern Rock, the little isle west of Saltreach. He grumbled that his chart is out of date.
  2. Stage 1 Keeper Brannoc wants three landmarks charted: the Gull Stacks off Lantern Rock, the bell on Tideholm, and the light on Saltreach's cape. I chart a landmark by sailing near it.
  3. Stage 2 I charted the Gull Stacks, the Tideholm bell and the Saltreach light for Keeper Brannoc. He gave me the last keeper's spyglass.
