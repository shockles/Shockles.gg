Bog oak
ExamineGrown fat and crooked on wet feet, hung with moss.
OptionsChop down
IDstree_bog

Bog oak is scenery.

Woodcutting

  • Woodcutting level 50, 125 xp
  • Gives Bog oak logs
  • Lasts 60 seconds of chopping (only while someone chops; it heals when left), then falls on the next log
  • Grows back in about 8 seconds
