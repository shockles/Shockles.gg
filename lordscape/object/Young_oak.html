Young oak is scenery.

Woodcutting

  • Woodcutting level 15, 37.5 xp
  • Gives Oak logs
  • Lasts 27 seconds of chopping (only while someone chops; it heals when left), then falls on the next log
  • Grows back in about 8 seconds
