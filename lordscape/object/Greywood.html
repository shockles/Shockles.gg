Greywood is scenery.

Woodcutting

  • Woodcutting level 45, 100 xp
  • Gives Greywood logs
  • Lasts 60 seconds of chopping (only while someone chops; it heals when left), then falls on the next log
  • Grows back in about 35 seconds
