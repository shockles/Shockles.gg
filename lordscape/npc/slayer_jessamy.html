Captain Jessamy Thorne runs Slayer equipment.

Slayer master

Gives Slayer tasks to players of combat level 1 or higher; only kills on the Drift's and the Drownings' waters count, with a streak of her own, 25 points a task (milestones: 125 on the 10th, 375 on the 50th, 625 on the 100th, 875 on the 250th, 1,250 on the 1000th). Blocking a task here costs 100 points. Rewards: The Taskmasters' Rewards.
