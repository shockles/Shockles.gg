Notes

Evan is a leprechaun who guards the Pot of gold at the rainbow's end, in the meadows east of Ashwick Quarry. He wanders a small patch round his pot and watches for thieves up to six tiles away.

  • Steal from the pot while he can see you and you're caught: stunned, hurt, and Evan comes after you. He can't be attacked.
  • Wait until he's wandered off out of sight (or round behind something) before you steal.
  • Rob him and he won't talk to you for a while.
