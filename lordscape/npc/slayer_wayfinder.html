Shop

Wayfinder Sefton Hale runs Slayer equipment.

Slayer master

Gives Slayer tasks to players of combat level 75 or higher; every task at a place, and sandseal keys for his coffer, 18 points a task (milestones: 90 on the 10th, 270 on the 50th, 450 on the 100th, 630 on the 250th, 900 on the 1000th). Blocking a task here costs 80 points. Rewards: The Taskmasters' Rewards.
